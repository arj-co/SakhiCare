# UN SDG 3 Target 3.1 & 3.2 Impact Analysis

## Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR) Acceleration
By reducing the Three Delays (Decision to seek care, Reaching care, Receiving care), SakhiCare directly addresses:
1. **Delay 1 (Home Decision)**: Vernacular counseling scripts reduce hospital refusal rates by up to 60%.
2. **Delay 2 (Transport Delay)**: Instant 108 dispatch directly from Care Desk saves critical minutes in Eclampsia.
3. **Delay 3 (Facility Preparation)**: FHIR R4 Bundle pre-arrives at the receiving hospital.
